01Why this matters

Robots will learn from the world as it is. That world is physical, operational and messy.

Simulation and lab setups take models far. But systems that act in the physical world also need examples of real work: real tools, real procedures, real variation in light, space, clutter and people.

Getting that material means operating inside worksites that were never built for data collection. It is an operations problem before it is a data problem. OperumData exists to structure that bridge in Brazil.

Client acceptance criteria

Captured time and approved material are not the same measure.

The value of an operation is not only in volume captured, but in producing material that matches the protocol and the criteria the client defines.

  • Operational compliance with the protocol
  • Verification of field execution
  • Identification of recurring failures
  • Communication and process correction
  • Coordination of re-captures when needed

Final technical acceptance of the material remains based on the client's criteria.

08FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

01Have you already run data capture projects for Physical AI?

No. OperumData is in an experimental phase. We are structuring the operating capability and are open to pilot projects. We do not present past AI capture work because there is none to present yet.

02Do you only use your own employees?

No. Depending on the project, participants can be identified in partner companies and third-party environments. OperumData qualifies and coordinates participation according to the defined requirements.

03Do the worksites need to belong to OperumData?

No. We can run operations in third-party locations, as long as they are compatible with the protocol and collection is authorised.

04Can you recruit new participants?

Yes. Sourcing follows the profile, experience, location and activities the project requires.

05Do you provide the hardware?

It depends on the scope. We can operate with hardware and applications provided by the client. Other needs are assessed while defining the project.

06Do you only work with construction?

No. Construction is one possible category. We can also develop operations in workshops, maintenance, carpentry, small industrial operations, restaurants, commercial kitchens, retail, cleaning and technical installations.

07Can you commit to weekly capacity before reviewing the project?

Not responsibly. Capacity depends on the protocol, tasks, environments, hardware, logistics and acceptance criteria. When needed, a pilot produces an estimate based on real execution.

08Where do you operate?

The initial operation is based in Rio Grande do Sul. New locations are evaluated according to each project's scope, timeline and logistics.

09How are privacy, authorizations and consent handled?

Requirements are defined for each operation. Worksite authorizations, consented participation, areas permitted for capture and applicable privacy requirements are organised before collection begins.
